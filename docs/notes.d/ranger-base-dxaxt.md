# ranger-base-dxaxt — the account's 199 characters, and the pin that gives them back

`ranger-base-uu303`'s reading found the lever and this is the landing: one
function's silence tail was the member set's whole line budget, and taking it
out is what made the necessary condition landable.

Every number below is MEASURED 2026-11-22 on this box (linux 7.2.15-1-omarchy,
go1.27.3, 16 cores, `TMPDIR` unset) at tip `3ef2d977`, or DERIVED from the
source by arithmetic this entry spells out. `ranger-base-uu303`'s own figures
were taken at `e5221de6`, which is BEFORE `ranger-base-momix` widened arm 2's
key — so its "32 account messages" is the old narrow key and everything here
reads the widened one, **65 messages in 8 of 24 member files**. The two
readings agree everywhere they overlap: the widest literal preamble is 233 at
`bwrapseatstorelive_test.go:751` in both.

## WHAT WENT, and it is 172 of the 199

`bgSeatAccount` (`internal/posse/bwrapgitrefslive_test.go`) returns `<status>`
plus one of two tails. The silence tail was a fixed 199-character literal:

```
, and the seat said NOTHING — so that status is the whole account: no error
from the command and no bwrap refusal, which is the shape of a seat ended from
outside rather than one the wall turned away
```

It is now `, and the seat said NOTHING` — **27 characters**. What the clause
said is not evidence, and two things decided it rather than taste:

- **IT IS SAID WHERE IT IS TRUE.** Silence plus a supervisor's signal is
  routed to ADR 0097 D3's abstention (`bgSeatAbstainIfOutside`), and
  `bgSeatAbstainAccount`'s own sentence carries the ended-from-outside
  reading — "the seat was ended by a signal from outside the sandbox before it
  could answer". The tail was saying it a second time on the one path where it
  holds, and spending the abstention line's own 300 to do it.
- **AND IT CONTRADICTED THE STATUS IN FRONT OF IT EVERYWHERE ELSE.** What
  still reaches a bead carrying this account as a RED is a status the house
  reads as the wall's or git's own answer — `exit status 128` at `cannot lock
  ref` is the row `ranger-base-axt1f` exists to keep apart from `signal:
  killed`, and SIGSYS's 159 is the wall's doing by that function's own
  comment. `exit status 128, and the seat said NOTHING — … no error from the
  command …` is wrong and not merely redundant. This is the half
  `ranger-base-dxaxt`'s own description did not have: the bead read the clause
  as reasoning, and it is reasoning that had gone stale under
  `bgSeatEndedFromOutside`.

`bgSeatEndedFromOutside`'s comment quoted the clause as something the account
"already NAMES"; it now says where that reading went instead. The behaviour
pin over the function (`bwrapgitrefsaccount_qa_test.go`) asks for `NOTHING` in
the silence account and for no newline in any of them, and both still hold.

## THE BUDGET, DERIVED

| term | where it is read | today |
| --- | --- | --- |
| the slice | `evidence()`'s `text[:300]` in `scripts/posse-wall-check` | 300 |
| the account | the one return of `bgSeatAccount` that is one identifier plus literals, at the shortest literal its body assigns that identifier | `exit 0` (6) + 27 = **33** |
| the prefix | `<basename>:<line>: `, which go test writes and `evidence()` keeps (`line.strip()` strips indentation and nothing else) | 32 at `:751` |
| the preamble | the format string's literal text before the account's own verb, first line only, every interpolated verb counted as ZERO | 201 at `:751` |

`prefix + preamble` is what the arm reports and what `ranger-base-uu303`
tabulated; its widest is **233**, at `bwrapseatstorelive_test.go:751` in both
readings. The arm logs that figure on every run, which is the number to watch.

Nothing is typed and nothing is fitted. The rule is
`prefix + preamble + account <= slice`, in RUNES — `evidence()` is Python and
slices a `str`, so the budget is code points, and the member set's prose is
em-dash heavy.

| the silence tail | the account | a message's budget | over budget |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 199 (before) | 205 | 95 | **28 of 65** |
| 27 (after) | 33 | 267 | **0 of 65** |

34 characters of headroom on the widest literal preamble in the set. The 28
included every `CONTROL:` message whose preamble is the complete claim ADR
0097 D3 wants on line 1 — correct, and unlandable without 28 prose edits,
which is why the lever was the account.

## WHY THE STATUS FLOOR IS `exit 0` AND NOT THE WIDEST STATUS

`ranger-base-dxaxt`'s description derived the budget from `exit status 128`
(15 characters), giving 86 rather than 95. That number is not in the source:
`exit 0` is the one status `bgSeatAccount` spells, and the rest come from
`err.Error()`. The pin reads what the source says and the arithmetic stays a
floor, because every status a real failure produces is longer — MEASURED,
`signal: killed` is 14 and `exit status 128` is 15. Taking the wider figure
would have been a measured number typed into a pin, and a pin that types a
number is the register this file's head comment refuses.

## WHAT THE PIN IS, AND WHAT IT IS NOT

`internal/treepins/wallmemberaccount_qa_test.go` gains two arms, and arms 1
and 2 now read the account-message key through one shared walk
(`wmaAccountMessages`) rather than two copies of it:

- **arm 6**, `TestQAEveryWallMemberAccountFitsInsideTheEvidenceBudget` — the
  rule above over the member set. It is **NECESSARY and not sufficient**:
  three messages blow the 300 on interpolated fixture paths of 124-174
  characters (`ranger-base-bj1hh`), and in ADR 0097 D1's venue every
  `t.TempDir()` path is +50. Sufficiency needs a runtime render venue, which
  `ranger-base-uu303` records rather than files.
- **arm 7**, `TestQATheWallMemberBudgetReadersMatchWhatTheyClaim` — arm 6's
  readers over planted input, no member set and no venue, the way arm 3 holds
  arms 1 and 2.

It is in no `twdTreepinsExcluded` row, so `make treepins-check` — the
complement door — reaches it with no hand-written Makefile entry (ADR 0098
D4). 111s on this box with the two new arms in it.

## THE BOTH-WAYS READING, ten mutants one at a time

`go test -count=1` throughout. The first run of this battery was green on six
mutants and the reason was the test cache: arm 6 reads two files that are not
in `internal/treepins`'s build, so a mutant in either left a cached PASS in
place. Anyone re-running it needs `-count=1`.

| mutant | what red |
| --- | --- |
| control, unmutated | nothing (exit 0) |
| 1. the 199-character tail restored | arm 6, **28 messages**, each naming its file, line and what the bead would get |
| 2. the tail grows 35 past the widest preamble | arm 6, 1 message (`:751`) |
| 3. `evidence()` slices 200 | arm 6: the budget reader refuses the disagreement AND 4 messages |
| 4. `evidence()`'s two halves disagree | arm 6: the budget reader refuses; 0 messages, the budget unchanged |
| 5. the preamble counted in bytes | arm 7, on the em-dash row |
| 6. both account branches fixed-width | arm 6's reader REFUSES rather than guessing a width; arm 5 reds too |
| 7. the status floor is the longest literal | arm 7 (reads 53, want 33) |
| 8. the prefix counted as zero | arm 7 |
| 8b. the prefix is the whole path, not the basename | arm 7 |
| 9. the shared account-message walk returns nothing | arm 6's control AND arm 2's five floors |

**Mutant 8 is why `wmaLinePrefix` is a named reader.** With the prefix term
replaced by a constant 0 the member set still fits inside 267 and arm 6 stays
green — the tree does not witness that term — so the claim is held where a
fixture can check it instead of by a bound nothing can red. That is
`ranger-base-8752f`'s ruling one arm over, in this same file.

Mutant 6 is the one worth reading twice: it makes the words branch literal
too, so the function has two fixed-width answers and no single one to budget
for. The reader says so and stops. It never guesses, which is the whole of
`ranger-base-uu303`'s warning about a number nobody measured.
